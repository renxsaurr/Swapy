# Home Swap Check-In and Checkout

## Purpose
Guide guests and hosts through arrival and departure while recording useful milestones.

## Before Arrival
- Show confirmed dates, guest count, house rules, and support contact.
- Host shares access instructions privately at the agreed time.
- Both parties can complete an optional timestamped condition checklist.

## Check-In
1. Guest confirms arrival in the transaction.
2. If access fails, show **I’m locked out** and open priority support.
3. Guest can report a serious mismatch or safety concern immediately.

## Check-Out
1. Guest confirms departure.
2. Host completes the checkout checklist.
3. Both can add timestamped photos or notes.
4. Host submits any damage concern by the policy deadline; guest can respond.

Check-in and checkout record user-confirmed milestones. They are not continuous location tracking or proof that a home is safe.

## Justification
Simple arrival and departure confirmations give both parties a timeline and a place to report urgent issues. Optional checklists can help document condition without claiming that SWAPY inspected the home.
