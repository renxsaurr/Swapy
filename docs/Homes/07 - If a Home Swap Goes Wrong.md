# If a Home Swap Goes Wrong

## Purpose
Route home-related reports, transaction deadlines, disputes, and urgent safety issues for every home transaction type. Apply the location profile for evidence, response windows, remedies, and completion.

## Issue Types
- Guest cannot access the home
- Home materially differs from listing
- Safety or urgent maintenance concern
- Host or guest does not arrive
- Property damage or missing items
- Cancellation or date dispute
- Identity or authority-to-list concern
- Suspected misleading verification label or document mismatch
- Property sale/trade due-diligence or closing issue
- One-sided completion of an ownership trade

## Support Flow
1. User reports the issue from the active home transaction.
2. For immediate danger, direct them to local emergency services.
3. For a lockout, contact host and backup contact through the support workflow.
4. Ask both parties for relevant messages, photos, timestamps, and agreement details.
5. Give the other party a response window and preserve the listing version, rule-profile version, agreement versions, and submitted evidence.
6. Support routes property sale/trade cases to trained reviewers; do not treat ordinary support notes as legal/closing approval.
7. Support records the decision, evidence basis, next actions, responsible owner, and whether binding activity remains paused.

## Damage Claims
Require the location-profile deadline, before/after evidence where available, itemized costs, and a response from the other party. Do not automatically forfeit a deposit based on one party’s report. Damage deposits remain unavailable unless the enabled profile and payment provider explicitly approve that flow.

## Property Transfer Problems
- If closing evidence is missing or rejected, keep the property transaction open for support review and do not mark it complete.
- For an ownership trade, track both transfer tracks independently. If one transfer completes and the other does not, flag the trade as **Partially completed — support review required**. Preserve both records, pause any remaining platform-controlled step where permitted, and route both parties to the location-appropriate remedy process. Do not claim SWAPY can reverse a legally completed transfer.
- Do not edit signed agreements or evidence. Correct errors through versioned addenda or new evidence entries with an audit trail.

## Justification
Home issues can involve safety and access, so they need a prominent, faster support route than an ordinary item dispute. Evidence and a response opportunity reduce unsupported damage claims.
