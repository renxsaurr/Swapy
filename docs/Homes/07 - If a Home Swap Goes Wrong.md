# If a Home Swap Goes Wrong

## Purpose
Route lockouts, material listing mismatches, safety concerns, and damage claims to the right support path.

## Issue Types
- Guest cannot access the home
- Home materially differs from listing
- Safety or urgent maintenance concern
- Host or guest does not arrive
- Property damage or missing items
- Cancellation or date dispute

## Support Flow
1. User reports the issue from the active home transaction.
2. For immediate danger, direct them to local emergency services.
3. For a lockout, contact host and backup contact through the support workflow.
4. Ask both parties for relevant messages, photos, timestamps, and agreement details.
5. Give the other party a response window.
6. Support records a resolution and any next steps.

## Damage Claims
Require a timely report, before/after evidence where available, itemized costs, and a response from the other party. Do not automatically forfeit a deposit based on one party’s report. The launch flow should not collect a damage deposit until its payment and legal handling are approved.

## Justification
Home issues can involve safety and access, so they need a prominent, faster support route than an ordinary item dispute. Evidence and a response opportunity reduce unsupported damage claims.
