# Create Home Swap Listing

## Purpose
Let a host describe a home and the dates they may exchange with another member.

## UI Flow
Use focused steps: Home details → Availability and preferences → Privacy and preview.

### Home details
Collect:
- Listing title and photos
- Approximate area, not exact public address
- Home type, capacity, sleeping arrangements
- Amenities and accessibility information
- House rules, pets, and known limitations or hazards

### Availability and preferences
Collect:
- Available dates or date ranges
- Minimum and maximum stay, if applicable
- Guest count
- Whether the host is seeking a reciprocal home swap or is open to another approved arrangement
- Optional destination or home preferences

### Privacy and preview
Explain what is public and what is shared only after confirmation. Let the host preview and publish or save a draft.

## Launch Rules
- Home listings are for swaps/stays, not sale or ordinary rent.
- Do not require a monetary valuation.
- Do not publish exact address, door code, or private contact details.
- Require the host to confirm they have authority to offer the property.

## Justification
Guests need enough detail to judge fit and dates, while the host needs control over sensitive location and access information. Calling the experience a home swap keeps it distinct from renting or purchasing property.
