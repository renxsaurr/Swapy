# Short Stays and Home Swaps

## Purpose
Define the short-duration occupancy flow for reciprocal home swaps and any other short-stay arrangement explicitly enabled by the location profile.

## Before Binding Actions
1. Confirm the selected city/region has an Approved and Enabled short-stay profile.
2. Verify participant identity and host authority to offer the stay as required by that profile.
3. Complete required listing review, safety disclosures, dates, guest limits, and local documents.
4. If any required check is missing or profile becomes Suspended, permit general discovery only if the profile allows it; block binding requests, agreements, and payments.

## Negotiation and Agreement
- Pin the home listing/version, exact requested dates, duration, number of guests, house rules, accessibility needs, pets, cancellation terms, and any specifically approved costs.
- A reciprocal swap links both homes and represents each stay leg separately, including dates and responsible host/guest parties.
- Use the approved short-stay agreement version for this location. Do not use the Items Swap Contract or a rental/sale agreement.
- Confirm dates only after all required participants sign the same version and required checks pass.

## Arrival, Stay, and Completion
Use [[06 - Home Swap Check-In and Checkout|Home Swap Check-In and Checkout]]. Record separate arrival/departure milestones for each stay leg. Share exact address and access instructions privately at the allowed stage. A check-in confirmation is user-reported and is not a guarantee of property condition or safety.

## Payment and Claims
Only show charges, cost-sharing, deposits, and refunds enabled by the location profile and approved provider flow. Do not promise escrow or deposit recovery. Route access, misrepresentation, damage, and cancellation cases to [[07 - If a Home Swap Goes Wrong|If a Home Swap Goes Wrong]].

## Justification
Short stays focus on dates, occupants, access, and house rules. A dedicated flow prevents these booking details from being confused with rental terms or legal ownership transfers.
