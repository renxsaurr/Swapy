# Buyer Finds a Home

## Purpose
Help a member find a home swap that fits their destination, dates, and household.

## Discovery UI
Search by destination or area, available dates, guest count, home type, and accessibility needs. Let users browse approximate areas before identity verification.

### Home card
Show a main photo, title, approximate area, availability summary, capacity, and a compact swap indicator.

### Home detail page
Show the gallery, amenities, sleeping arrangements, rules, pets, known limitations, host profile, review summary, and available dates. Keep the exact address and access details private.

## Buyer Actions
- Save home
- Ask host a question
- Request dates
- Propose a reciprocal home swap by linking their own home listing

Requesting dates is not a confirmed booking. The dates are reserved only after both parties agree and sign the home-swap agreement.

## Marketing Decision
Feature destination and date-fit recommendations, not price-based sorting. Let members save searches and opt into availability alerts.

## Justification
For a home swap, destination and date compatibility matter more than a nightly price. Showing approximate location supports early discovery while protecting the host’s address.
