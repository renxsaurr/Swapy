# Buyer Finds a Home

## Purpose
Help a member find a home swap that fits their destination, dates, and household.

## Discovery UI
Require the user to choose **Short stay**, **Long-term rental**, **Property sale**, or **Ownership trade**. Search by destination/area and type-specific filters (dates and guest count for stays; rent and term for rentals; price and property characteristics for sale/trade). The location profile determines which filters and actions are available. Let users browse approximate areas before identity verification where permitted.

### Home card
Show a main photo, title, approximate area, availability or listing status, relevant type-specific terms, and a clear transaction-type badge. Never make a short-stay card look like a rental or sale listing.

### Home detail page
Show the gallery and details relevant to the selected transaction type, the party’s profile, review summary, and precisely worded verification labels. Keep exact address, access details, and documents private. Explain which checks were performed and which facts remain unverified.

## Buyer Actions
- Save home
- Ask host a question
- For short stays: request dates or propose a reciprocal home swap by linking an eligible home listing.
- For long-term rental: inquire/apply using the location-approved workflow.
- For sale: submit an inquiry or offer under the location-approved purchase workflow.
- For ownership trade: propose a trade that identifies both properties and routes to the two-transfer workflow.

An inquiry, application, or offer is not a booking or transfer. Binding actions are enabled only when that transaction type’s location profile is Approved and Enabled, and only after its required checks and agreement steps.

## Marketing Decision
Feature destination and date-fit recommendations, not price-based sorting. Let members save searches and opt into availability alerts.

## Justification
For a home swap, destination and date compatibility matter more than a nightly price. Showing approximate location supports early discovery while protecting the host’s address.
