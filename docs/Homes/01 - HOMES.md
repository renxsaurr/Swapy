# Homes

## Purpose
Support four distinct home transaction types with transaction-specific discovery, checks, agreements, money handling, evidence, and completion rules.

## Home Transaction Types
1. **Short stays:** temporary occupancy for agreed dates, including reciprocal home swaps where enabled by the market profile.
2. **Long-term rentals:** occupancy under a location-approved rental flow with required screening, disclosures, agreement, payments, and local steps.
3. **Property sales:** transfer of a property through the locally required due-diligence and closing process. An in-app signature alone does not transfer ownership.
4. **Trades of property ownership:** two linked property transfer tracks, each with its own requirements and evidence. Overall completion waits for both transfers.

Each type has a separate transaction flow. Do not reuse the ordinary Items Swap Contract for home occupancy or property ownership transfers. Do not enable binding requests, bookings, contracts, or payments until the applicable location profile is Approved and Enabled.

## Shared Safeguards
- Verify identity as required by the enabled location and transaction profile; separately verify authority to list, rent, sell, or transfer each property.
- Review the listing and documents under the applicable profile. Tell users exactly which facts SWAPY checked and which it did not.
- Keep exact addresses, identity documents, title/deed records, access instructions, and private disclosures hidden from public pages and accessible only to authorized transaction participants and staff.
- Provide report, block, transaction support, and urgent safety routes across every home flow.
- Mark a transaction complete only when its transaction-specific completion evidence is reviewed and recorded.
- If a market is unreviewed, suspended, or has conflicting requirements, block binding actions and payments for that transaction type. Discovery may remain available if the location profile allows it.

## End-to-End Pattern
Select home transaction type → verify location profile is enabled → create and review listing → discovery and inquiry → identity/authority checks → negotiate type-specific terms → complete due diligence and disclosures → sign the correct agreement → complete approved payment/closing/access steps → review completion evidence → complete or route issue to support.

## Marketing Decision
Label every home card and promotion with the exact transaction type—short stay, long-term rental, property sale, or ownership trade. Do not imply that SWAPY verified title, legal compliance, property condition, or safety unless that precise check was performed and recorded.

## Justification
Home occupancy and real-property transfers have distinct risks and requirements. Separate flows avoid implying that a simple marketplace signature books a lawful rental or transfers property title.
