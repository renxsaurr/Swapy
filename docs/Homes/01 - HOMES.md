# Homes

## Purpose
Help members arrange a home swap or stay for specific dates, with privacy, identity, and access handled carefully.

## Launch Scope
Homes means peer-to-peer home swaps/stays for agreed dates. It does not mean property sales, ordinary rentals, or real-estate brokerage. Do not collect rent or deposits until the legal, insurance, and payment model is separately approved.

## Flow
Create home listing → browse destination and dates → request or propose a swap → negotiate guest count and terms → verify participants → sign home-swap agreement → share private access details → check in and stay → check out → resolve any claim → review.

## Marketing Decision
Promote destination, date availability, and the experience of exchanging homes. Do not describe homes as inspected or guaranteed safe unless a real program supports that claim.

## Justification
A home stay has different risks and information needs from an item trade. Separating this module from property sales and rentals keeps the launch proposition understandable and avoids implying a real-estate transaction model.
