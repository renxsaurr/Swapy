# Services

## Purpose
Connect customers with people offering defined services, with clear scope, timing, and exchange terms.

## Service Formats
Providers choose which they accept:
- Service for money
- Service for another service
- Service plus cash

## Flow
Create service listing → customer discovers provider → ask/request service → agree scope, schedule, and terms → sign/confirm → pay if applicable → provider delivers → customer confirms → review or report a problem.

## Launch Rule
Regulated or high-risk service categories require separate eligibility and compliance review before listings or payments are enabled.

## Marketing Decision
Help providers describe a concrete outcome and availability. Build repeat use through reviews and referrals, not unverified “expert” claims.

## Justification
A service is not a physical item: users need to agree on scope, timing, and completion before money or reviews make sense. A structured service brief reduces misunderstandings and supports repeat bookings.
