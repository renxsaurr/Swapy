# Confirm Service and Payment

## Purpose
Confirm the provider, customer, agreed scope, schedule, and any payment before work begins.

## Confirmation Screen
Show the final service brief, provider and customer identities, schedule, price or exchange terms, fees, cancellation terms, and support route.

Both parties confirm the same version. For paid services, payment occurs only through an approved provider flow and after the amount and payer are clear. Service swaps without cash require no SWAPY payment.

## Statuses
Awaiting confirmation → Payment needed / No payment required → Confirmed → Cancelled.

If payment fails, show retry and cancellation deadlines. Do not describe processed funds as escrow unless the payment arrangement is approved for that purpose.

## Justification
A separate confirmation step makes the service commitment and payment visible before delivery. It helps prevent a quote or chat message from being mistaken for a booked service.
