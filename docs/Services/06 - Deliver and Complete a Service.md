# Deliver and Complete a Service

## Purpose
Track the agreed service from scheduled start through customer confirmation.

## Flow
1. Show the confirmed schedule, scope, and preparation checklist.
2. Provider marks **Started** and later **Work submitted** or **Completed**.
3. Customer reviews the result and selects **Accept**, **Report a problem**, or **Request an agreed correction**.
4. If accepted, mark the service complete and enable reviews.
5. If the customer does not respond by the stated review deadline, send reminders and follow the clearly displayed completion policy.

For multi-session work, show each agreed milestone separately. Do not mark the entire service complete when only one milestone has been delivered.

## Completion Screen
Show the scope, schedule, payment/exchange status, completion date, review action, and help link.

## Justification
Milestone visibility prevents confusion about what has been delivered. Completion confirmation is the right moment to close the transaction and ask for a review.
