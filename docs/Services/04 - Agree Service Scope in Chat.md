# Agree Service Scope in Chat

## Purpose
Turn a customer request into a specific, reviewable service proposal.

## Chat and Service Brief
Pin the service listing and show a structured brief with:
- Requested outcome and deliverables
- Included work and exclusions
- Schedule, duration, and location/remote method
- Price, service swap, or service-plus-cash terms
- Materials or customer preparation required
- Cancellation and rescheduling terms

Provider may accept, ask a question, decline, or counter. Each counter creates a new version. Free-text chat does not change the brief until the structured terms are updated.

## Ready State
When both users agree, mark **Terms ready for confirmation** and continue to [[05 - Confirm Service and Payment|Confirm Service and Payment]].

## Justification
A structured scope helps both parties distinguish an agreed deliverable from an informal conversation. It also gives support a clear reference if the service is incomplete or disputed.
