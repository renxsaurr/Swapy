# Buyer Finds a Service

## Purpose
Help a customer find a provider whose service, schedule, location, and terms fit their need.

## Discovery
Search by service category, location or remote availability, desired date, price range, and provider availability.

### Service card
Show service title, one-line outcome, starting price if provided, local/remote label, and rating summary when available.

### Service detail
Show included work, exclusions, duration, availability, location, accepted exchange formats, cancellation terms, provider profile, and relevant credentials.

## Customer Actions
- Request this service
- Ask a question
- Offer a service swap, if accepted
- Save provider/listing

A request is not confirmed until scope, schedule, and terms are agreed.

## Marketing Decision
Rank for relevance, availability, distance/remote fit, and quality signals. Clearly label paid promotion and do not treat a paid placement as proof of qualification.

## Justification
A service card should support quick scanning; the detail page explains the actual scope. Distinguishing starting price from a confirmed quote avoids misleading customers.
