# Create Service Listing

## Purpose
Let a provider explain what they do, what is included, and how customers can request it.

## Required Listing Content
- Service title and category
- Description of the outcome and included work
- What is excluded or requires a separate quote
- Price or “Open to offers,” if paid work is accepted
- Duration or expected completion time
- Availability and service area or remote option
- Accepted formats: money, service swap, or service plus cash
- Cancellation and rescheduling terms

Add qualifications only where relevant and verifiable. Ask category-specific questions when needed. Do not enable regulated/high-risk categories until their rules are reviewed.

## UI Flow
Provider details → Service scope → Schedule and location → Offer formats → Preview and publish.

## Justification
Customers need to know what they will receive and when before they request a service. Clear exclusions and cancellation terms reduce disputes and make good providers easier to compare.
