# On-Site Garage Sale Transactions

## Purpose
Make event purchases and trades quick while recording which item changed hands.

## Flow
1. Buyer opens an event item or scans its QR code.
2. Buyer chooses the available format: cash purchase, item swap, or item plus cash.
3. If swapping, show a compact offer form and confirm exact item and cash direction.
4. Both parties inspect the item and review terms.
5. Each taps **Confirm handover**.
6. Mark the event item sold/traded and update the host inventory.

A host may offer a short reservation, recommended default 10 minutes. Show when it expires and whether it holds the current listed price.

Offline cash can be acknowledged by both parties, but SWAPY cannot guarantee or reverse that payment.

## Justification
A QR entry and short transaction form reduce waiting and prevent duplicate claims on event inventory. The flow still confirms the exact item and any cash instead of treating an in-person conversation as a recorded sale.
