# SWAPY

## What SWAPY Is
SWAPY is a peer-to-peer marketplace where people sell and trade items, arrange home swaps, offer services, and discover temporary local Garage Sales.

**Brand promise:** Turn what you have into what you want.

## Common User Journey
Discover something useful → view its detail page → make an offer or request → negotiate the terms → confirm the agreement → complete the handover or service → inspect/confirm completion → review and return.

## Core Business Decisions
- Items can be offered for sale, swap, or item plus cash.
- Homes launch as home swaps/stays for agreed dates, not property sales or ordinary rentals.
- Services have a defined scope, schedule, and completion confirmation.
- Garage Sales are temporary local events with their own inventory and handover flow.
- My Store is a public storefront and private listing manager, not a cart or separate checkout.
- SWAPY does not appraise items or promise equal trade value.
- Paid promotion buys disclosed additional visibility; it does not guarantee results.
- Essential discovery, messaging, and safe transaction steps should not be reserved for paid tiers.

## Justification
A clear product map makes SWAPY understandable as one marketplace with several listing experiences. Keeping a shared account, discovery system, chat, trust rules, and My Store creates cross-category discovery while letting each transaction use a workflow suited to its real-world handover.
