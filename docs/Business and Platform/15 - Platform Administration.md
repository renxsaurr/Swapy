# Platform Administration

Staff need one admin area to support the whole marketplace. Access should match each person’s job.

## What staff manage

- Accounts and member reports
- Listings and events
- Safety reviews and moderation
- Support cases and transactions
- Payments and refunds, where supported
- Location profiles and approved transaction rules

## Staff roles

Create separate access for:

- Platform administrators
- Marketplace operations
- Trust and safety reviewers
- Customer support
- Finance staff
- Location-rule editors and approvers

Give each role only the information and actions it needs. For example, support staff may need to read a case without changing location rules or payment records.

## Review and approval

Location-rule editors prepare changes. A different approved person reviews and enables them. Record who made and approved each change and when it takes effect.

Staff must not silently change signed agreements, member messages, payment results, or transaction evidence. Corrections need a reason and an audit record.

## Support case handling

1. Record the report, people involved, transaction, and evidence.
2. Assign the case to the right team.
3. Ask for missing information and keep both sides informed.
4. Record the decision, reason, and any action taken.
5. Close the case when resolved, or keep it open and explain the next step.

See [Support and Moderation Operations](10%20-%20Support%20and%20Moderation%20Operations.md) for the case process and [Global Location Rules](14%20-%20Global%20Location%20Rules.md) for rule approvals.
