# Promotions and Memberships

## Purpose
Define how SWAPY earns revenue while keeping the marketplace useful and trustworthy for free members.

## Revenue Principles
- Essential browsing, messaging, offers, safety controls, and transaction records remain available to all users.
- Paid plans sell scale, convenience, insights, and promotional tools.
- Paid placement is labeled and never guarantees a transaction.
- Do not use arbitrary monthly trade caps or item-value caps that suppress marketplace liquidity.

## Recommended Model
Use two optional revenue paths:
1. One-off boosts for occasional listers and event hosts.
2. Monthly membership for frequent listers who want higher limits, richer media, analytics, and a small included boost allowance.

## Justification
An early peer-to-peer marketplace needs enough useful supply and active buyers. Charging for basic participation can reduce both sides of the market. Optional promotions and memberships monetize frequent use without making free users unable to trade.
