# Membership Tiers and Billing

## Purpose
Offer optional paid tools to active listers without restricting basic marketplace participation.

## Recommended Tier Shape

| Feature | Free | Plus | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Browse, message, make offers, and complete trades | Included | Included | Included |
| Basic My Store | Included | Included | Included |
| Active listings | Generous starter allowance | Higher allowance | Highest allowance |
| Photos/media | Enough for a useful listing | More photos | More photos and video where supported |
| Store insights | Basic activity | More detailed insights | Advanced insights |
| Promotion | Buy one-off boosts | Small monthly boost allowance | Larger monthly boost allowance |
| Garage Sale tools | Create and attend | More event insights | Multiple event/promotion tools |
| Support | Standard support | Standard support | Optional account help for business-like users |

## Limits Policy
- Do not cap the number of completed swaps per month.
- Do not limit item value by tier.
- Do not put home contact, buyer messaging, safety, disputes, or core transactions behind a subscription.
- Prefer generous active-listing limits over restrictive photo or offer limits.
- Never imply paid members are safer or more verified.

## Price Hypotheses
For initial willingness-to-pay research, test Plus around USD $9.99/month and Pro around USD $24.99/month. These are hypotheses, not approved prices. Final pricing depends on target market, provider fees, support costs, and user research.

## Billing UX
Show the plan, renewal amount/date, included benefits, cancellation path, and whether boosts renew. Use an optional short trial only if the billing start and charge are explicit. Provide a receipt and self-serve cancellation.

## Justification
Freemium keeps supply and demand accessible while paid features monetize users who list often. Scale and promotion are clearer paid benefits than restricting item value or completed trades.
