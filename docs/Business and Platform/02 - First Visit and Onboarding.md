# First Visit and Onboarding

## Purpose
Help visitors understand SWAPY and find a useful listing before requiring account creation.

## Public Landing Page
Header: SWAPY logo, search, location, Explore, Items, Homes, Services, Garage Sales, My Store, Log in, Join.

First screen:
- Search items, homes, services, or Garage Sales
- Actions to find an item, explore home swaps, find a service, find a Garage Sale, or create My Store
- Nearby listings and upcoming events
- New and trade-friendly offers
- Short explanation of sale, swap, and item-plus-cash
- Trust and safety information

Visitors may browse public listings and approximate locations. Hide private addresses, access details, and direct contact information.

## Signup Triggers
Allow browsing without signup. Ask users to create an account when they try to message, make an offer/request, save, follow a store, or publish.

Onboarding asks for display name, profile, location/service area, interests, and whether they want to browse or list. Verify a contact channel before transaction actions. Ask for additional identity verification only when required by the chosen transaction or risk policy.

## Notifications
Make saved-search, store-follow, event, and price-change notifications opt-in and configurable.

## Justification
Open browsing helps visitors understand the marketplace before committing to signup. Contextual signup prompts reduce early friction while verification remains available before users enter higher-trust workflows.
