# Growth and Marketing

## Purpose
Grow SWAPY through useful local inventory, repeat participation, and honest sharing loops.

## Brand Message
**Turn what you have into what you want.**
Explain that SWAPY supports selling and trading items, home swaps, services, and local Garage Sales.

## Growth Loops
- **My Store:** members share a storefront; visitors browse multiple offers and follow it.
- **Garage Sales:** hosts share event pages; attendees follow hosts and return for new events.
- **Trade Up Challenge:** optional story of completed item trades, with values labeled by source and privacy controls.
- **Reviews:** completed transactions create credible feedback and improve future confidence.
- **Saved searches:** relevant new listings bring opted-in users back.

## Lifecycle Messaging
- Activation: help a new member save a search, publish a complete listing, or follow a store.
- Consideration: notify on relevant matches, offers, and events the user opted into.
- Transaction: send deadlines and status updates needed to complete the exchange.
- Retention: invite completed users to review, relist, follow, or share.
- Referral: share listings, stores, events, and Trade Up cards.

## Marketing Guardrails
- Do not use fake scarcity, fake reviews, hidden subscription renewal, or guaranteed-value claims.
- Label paid placements.
- Avoid rewarding raw trade volume or inflated user-stated item values.
- Let users control notification categories and frequency.

## Success Measures
Measure retained users and completed transactions alongside disputes, abandoned offers, and safety incidents. See [[13 - Launch Plan and Success Metrics|Launch Plan and Success Metrics]].

## Justification
SWAPY’s strongest growth should come from members’ real inventory and local events. Shareable stores and events create acquisition; saved searches and reviews support return use without relying on deceptive urgency.
