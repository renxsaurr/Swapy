# Cross-Module Explore and Search

## Purpose
Provide one coherent discovery experience while letting members search each category on its own terms.

## Explore
The Explore page can include nearby items, home swaps, services, upcoming Garage Sales, followed stores, and relevant new listings.

## Category Search
Search supports:
- Items: category, condition, price, sale/swap/hybrid, distance, shipping/meetup
- Homes: destination, dates, guest count, amenities, accessibility
- Services: category, location/remote, date, availability, price
- Garage Sales: event date, neighborhood, distance, inventory, cash/trade preference

Use sorting and filters appropriate to each category. Do not show fake or sponsored results as organic relevance.

## Result Cards
Cards stay concise and category-specific. Full condition, terms, host/provider detail, and policies appear on the detail page. Paid placements receive a visible Sponsored label.

## Saved Searches
Let users save a query and choose which updates to receive. Provide pause and delete controls.

## Marketing Decision
Cross-category recommendations introduce users to the breadth of SWAPY, while dedicated category filters keep search useful. Do not let cross-selling obscure the user’s chosen task.

## Justification
A shared Explore surface helps users discover SWAPY’s range; specialized filters respect that an item, home, service, and event require different information.
