# Trust and Safety

## Purpose
Reduce preventable harm and give members clear controls and support across every module.

## Account and Identity
- Verify contact channels before initiating transactions.
- Apply identity checks when transaction type, value, behavior, or local requirements call for them.
- Show what a verification badge means and what it does not mean.
- Identity verification does not certify an item, home, service quality, or personal safety.

## Listing Safety
- Review listings against SWAPY rules and applicable provider/category restrictions.
- Provide report actions for counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, prohibited, or misleading offers.
- Remove or limit listings under a documented moderation policy.
- Require home hosts to confirm authority to offer the stay and disclose material limitations.

## Privacy and Personal Safety
- Keep exact home addresses and access codes private until the agreed stage.
- Encourage public meetup locations for item exchanges.
- Provide block, report, and support controls.
- Collect only location and identity details needed for the user’s chosen transaction.

## Reviews and Trust Signals
- Request reviews only after completed transactions.
- Allow reports and moderation for abusive or irrelevant reviews.
- Do not allow paid membership or promotion to create a trust badge.

## Incident Handling
Use [[10 - Support and Moderation Operations|Support and Moderation Operations]] for triage, evidence review, urgent issues, and appeals.

## Justification
Trust comes from specific, understandable controls—not a broad promise that every user or listing is safe. Explaining verification limits avoids false confidence while still giving members useful signals.
