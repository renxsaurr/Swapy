# Support and Moderation Operations

## Purpose
Give support staff consistent workflows for safety reports, disputes, listing review, and account actions.

## Intake and Triage
Prioritize:
1. Immediate safety, lockout, threat, or suspected fraud.
2. Active transaction issues with deadlines or money in process.
3. Listing quality, policy, and general account questions.

Every case receives a case ID, issue type, response deadline, assigned owner, and status.

## Evidence and Decision
Review the relevant listing snapshot, offer versions, signed terms, chat, payment status, tracking, photos, and both parties’ explanations. Explain the policy basis for the outcome. Allow one appeal for new evidence or a clear process error.

## Moderation Actions
Possible actions: request edits, reduce visibility, pause a listing, restrict offers, temporarily limit an account, or suspend an account. Apply proportionately and record the reason. Serious safety or legal matters use an escalation path.

## Service Expectations
Publish support hours and response targets. Do not promise a four-hour response or dedicated manager unless staffing and operations can reliably meet it. Safety access remains available to every tier.

## Justification
A structured queue helps SWAPY respond consistently and preserve a useful evidence trail. Triage by urgency protects users better than treating every support request as the same priority.
