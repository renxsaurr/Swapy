# If Something Goes Wrong

## Purpose
Give users a clear way to report a problem and give support enough information to review it.

## Report a Problem Entry
Add **Report a problem** to the transaction timeline and item inspection screen.

Ask the user to choose a reason:
- Other user has not shipped
- Item has not arrived
- Wrong item received
- Item is damaged or materially different from the listing
- Missing accessories
- Cash or payment issue
- Meetup did not happen
- Other

Let the user describe the problem and add photos, tracking details, or other relevant evidence.

## Support Flow
1. User submits the issue.
2. Show the case as **Under review** and confirm the next response deadline.
3. Notify the other user and let them respond.
4. Support reviews the contract, listing snapshot, chat, tracking, payment status, and evidence from both users.
5. Support explains the proposed outcome and the next steps.
6. Give each user one appeal opportunity if they have new evidence or believe the policy was applied incorrectly.

Pause unsettled payments or transfers where the approved payment flow allows it. Do not promise recovery of an item or money that SWAPY cannot control.

## Common Outcomes

### Item not shipped
- Contact the sender and give them a response window.
- Allow a short extension for a documented carrier or emergency issue.
- If unresolved, cancel under the stated terms and return recoverable funds through the payment provider’s approved process.
- Apply any bond consequence only if the bond terms and payment arrangement allow it.

### Item is wrong, damaged, or materially misrepresented
- Compare the received item with the signed contract and listing snapshot.
- If supported by evidence, offer a return and refund or exchange-back under the stated policy.
- A partial cash adjustment requires both users’ agreement or a policy-supported decision.

### Hybrid refund
- Address the physical item and the cash leg separately.
- Prefer exchange-back or return-and-refund.
- If cash has already settled, explain that refunding it may require a new payment or a provider-supported reversal.

## Case Statuses
- Issue submitted
- Waiting for the other user
- Under review
- More information needed
- Resolution offered
- Appealed
- Resolved
- Closed

## Justification
A structured issue form helps users report the relevant problem without searching through chat. Reviewing the signed terms and listing snapshot gives support a shared reference. Separating item remedies from cash remedies is especially important for hybrid trades, where the physical exchange and payment may not be reversible in the same way.
