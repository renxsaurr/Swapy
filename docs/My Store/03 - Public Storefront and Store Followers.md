# Public Storefront and Store Followers

## Purpose
Let visitors browse a member’s offers and follow updates they want to receive.

## Public Page
Show the profile, bio, approximate service area, reviews, relevant verification indicators, and tabs for Items, Homes, Services, and Garage Sales. Show only active and publicly visible offers.

Actions:
- Follow Store / Following
- Share Store
- Open listing or event
- Message member where allowed

## Follow Preferences
Let followers choose notifications for:
- New items
- Home-swap availability
- New services
- Garage Sale events
- Price changes

Never expose private home addresses or access information on a public store page.

## Marketing Decision
Shareable storefront links support organic acquisition. Topic-level follow controls preserve relevance and reduce notification fatigue.

## Justification
A storefront lets users explore a member’s broader inventory and return later. Separate follow preferences make the relationship useful without flooding followers with every update.
