# My Store

## Purpose
Give every member a public storefront and private tools to manage listings and events across SWAPY.

## Public Storefront
Show the member’s profile, approximate service area, reviews, verification indicators, active items, home-swap listings, services, and upcoming Garage Sales. Add **Follow Store** and **Share Store**.

Keep private addresses, access instructions, and contact details hidden. A store is a collection of offers, not a cart or separate checkout.

## Private Manager
Manage listings and events, incoming offers, messages, visibility, followers, and basic activity such as views, saves, and offers.

## Marketing Decision
My Store is a durable sharing destination. Members can bring visitors from social channels, and followers can opt into updates by content type.

## Justification
A storefront helps buyers browse more of a person’s offers after discovering one listing. Keeping checkout within each listing’s transaction flow avoids introducing a confusing second marketplace process.
