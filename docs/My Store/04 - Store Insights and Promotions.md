# Store Insights and Promotions

## Purpose
Help members understand whether their listings and events are reaching interested people.

## Insights
Show simple, defined measures:
- Listing or store views
- Saves/follows
- Offers or service requests
- Event follows and attendance intentions
- Completed transactions

Do not present views as guaranteed buyer interest or user-reported values as verified revenue.

## Promotion Actions
Allow eligible listings or events to be boosted with a preview of placement, audience, duration, price, and Sponsored label. Show results after the campaign ends.

## Justification
Useful metrics help owners improve photos and descriptions. Separating promotion purchase from transaction success avoids promising results the marketplace cannot control.
